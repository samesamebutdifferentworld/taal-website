When staying might suit you

The honest case

Nothing up front

Monthly software has no setup fee, so year one costs less. With taal you pay for setup before you see a saving, which usually comes in year two. Spreading it over 12 or 24 months keeps each month close to what you pay now.

Leave any month

Most are month to month. taal's setup is paid whether you use it for one year or ten, though it keeps running if you stop working with us.

Finding new clients

Mindbody's consumer app can bring you people who've never heard of you. taal doesn't list you anywhere. It's your own app, for the people who already know you.

A bigger company

The larger platforms have big support teams, a long track record and many integrations. taal is a small London business.

How it's worked out

What each side counts

taal's side counts setup paid at the start, about £30 a month for hosting and email, the care plan if you switch it on, and with your own apps, Apple's developer fee of about £79 a year and Google's one-off $25 (about £20).

Your side counts what you enter, plus VAT if you say their prices are before VAT. Card fees and SMS aren't counted on either side, as they're similar either way.

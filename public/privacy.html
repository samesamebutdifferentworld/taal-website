Privacy

This page covers this website. Studios using Taal have their own privacy notice for their members.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Our fonts are served from this site, so your visit isn't shared with Google or anyone else.

If you request a demo

The demo form opens your own email app with your details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send. We use what you send only to reply to you and build your demo, and we don't share it or add you to a mailing list.

Hosting

This site is hosted by our hosting provider, which processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing EMAIL. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

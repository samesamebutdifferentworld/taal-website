Browsing this site

No cookies, analytics or tracking

We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our fonts are served from this site, so your visit isn't shared with Google or anyone else. If you pick a kind of studio on the sample app, your browser remembers that choice on your own device so the page opens the same way next time. It isn't sent to us.

If you build a demo

What we use and why

To make your demo we need your email address. You can also give us your name and studio name, and choose a type of studio, colours, fonts, class names and a logo. We use these to build your demo, email you the links and follow up about it. We don't add you to a mailing list or sell or share your details.

The legal basis is that you asked us to do this before possibly becoming a client, and our legitimate interest in following up about a demo you asked for. You can tell us to stop at any time.

How long we keep it

30 days for demos, a year for details
  • Your demo, including any logo you uploaded, is deleted after 30 days.
  • Your contact details are deleted after a year, unless you become a client.

Services we use

Cloudflare and Resend

These companies process data for us, only to run the site and send emails:

  • Cloudflare hosts this site, the demo builder and the demos. It processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks.
  • Resend sends the emails with your demo links.

Both are US companies, so your data may be processed outside the UK. Where it is, it's protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK's data bridge with the US or approved contract terms.

Your rights

Copies, corrections and deletion

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, delete it, stop using it or send it to you in a usable format. Email hello@taal.solutions and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Setup guide: Your web address

Pointing your domain at your new app and site

Your app and website go on your own web address. If you already have one, it stays yours and stays where it's registered: we only move where it points. If you don't have one yet, buy one first from any registrar (123 Reg, Namecheap, GoDaddy and others).

  1. Find where your domain is registered

    It's wherever you pay for it each year: look for the renewal email. Sign in there and find the page called DNS, Nameservers or Manage domain.

  2. Add it to Cloudflare

    In your Cloudflare account (see the Cloudflare guide), choose Add a domain, enter your web address, and pick the Free plan. Cloudflare copies your existing settings, including your email, so nothing stops working. Check the list it shows: your email records (MX) should be there.

  3. Change the nameservers

    Cloudflare shows you two nameservers, such as anna.ns.cloudflare.com. At your registrar, replace the existing nameservers with these two and save. It can take from a few minutes to a day to switch over; Cloudflare emails you when it's done.

  4. Tell us when it's active

    Tick it off in your portal. We then point your app and website at it on go-live day, so your current website keeps working until then.

All done?Tick Your web address off under Accounts in your portal, and we'll take it from there. Stuck on a step? Reply to any of our emails or write to hello@taal.solutions.

Setup guide: Resend

Sends your booking emails from your own address

Booking confirmations, reminders and receipts go out from your own address, such as bookings@yourstudio.co.uk, through Resend. It's free for up to 3,000 emails a month; most studios stay on the free plan or its $20 plan.

  1. Sign up

    Go to resend.com/signup and create an account with your business email.

  2. Add your domain

    Open Domains, then Add domain, and enter your studio's web address (for example yourstudio.co.uk). Pick the region nearest you (Ireland is fine for the UK).

  3. Let it set up the DNS

    If your domain is on Cloudflare (see the domain guide), choose the option to set up the records with Cloudflare automatically and approve it. Otherwise, leave this step to us once you've added us below.

  4. Add us to your team

    Open Settings, then Team, and invite hello@taal.solutions. If your plan doesn't let you invite people, reply to any of our emails and we'll finish this together on a short screen share instead.

All done?Tick Resend off under Accounts in your portal, and we'll take it from there. Stuck on a step? Reply to any of our emails or write to hello@taal.solutions.

Setup guide: Apple Developer

Your app on the App Store, in your name

Apple checks your business before it lets you publish, and that can take a couple of weeks, so start this one first. It costs $99 a year (about £79), paid to Apple.

  1. Check what Apple needs from you

    If your studio is a limited company, you enrol as an organisation and your company name shows as the seller on the App Store. You need: your company's exact legal name, its D-U-N-S number (free, step 2), a website on your own domain, an email address on that domain, and the authority to sign for the company. If you're a sole trader or partnership, Apple only lets you enrol as an individual, so your own name shows as the seller: skip step 2.

  2. Get your D-U-N-S number

    Go to developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup and search for your company exactly as it appears at Companies House. If it's listed, note the nine-digit number. If not, choose to request one: Dun & Bradstreet emails it to you, usually within a week, sometimes longer. It's free.

  3. Turn on two-factor sign-in for your Apple Account

    Use the Apple Account you'll keep for the business (it can be your own). On an iPhone: Settings, your name, Sign-In & Security, Two-Factor Authentication. Apple won't let you enrol without it.

  4. Enrol

    The easiest way is the Apple Developer app on an iPhone or iPad: open it, tap Account, then Enroll Now. Or go to developer.apple.com/programs/enroll on a computer. Choose Organisation (or Individual if you're a sole trader), enter your details exactly as on your D-U-N-S record, and pay the yearly fee.

  5. Answer Apple's call or email

    Apple usually checks with a phone call or email that you can sign for the company. Once you're approved, you'll get an email saying welcome to the Apple Developer Program.

  6. Add us so we can publish your app

    Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com, then Users and Access, then the + button. Enter hello@taal.solutions, choose the Admin role, and send the invitation. That lets us set up your app, upload it and send it to Apple for review. You can remove us at any time.

All done?Tick Apple Developer off under Accounts in your portal, and we'll take it from there. Stuck on a step? Reply to any of our emails or write to hello@taal.solutions.

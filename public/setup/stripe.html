Setup guide: Stripe

Takes your members' payments and pays you

Stripe takes card and Direct Debit payments from your members and pays them into your bank account. There's no monthly fee: Stripe takes a small fee from each payment.

  1. Sign up

    Go to dashboard.stripe.com/register and create an account with your business email. If you already have a Stripe account for your studio, use that one and skip to step 3.

  2. Activate payments

    Stripe asks for your business type, company number (if you have one), your address, your own details as the owner, and the bank account for payouts. Have your ID ready: Stripe may ask for a photo of it. Activation is usually quick.

  3. Turn on two-step sign-in

    Settings, then Personal details, then Two-step authentication. Use an authenticator app.

  4. Turn on Direct Debit

    Settings, then Payment methods, then Bacs Direct Debit: turn it on. Stripe may ask a few more questions before it approves it. This is what lets members pay their memberships by Direct Debit.

  5. Add us to your team

    Settings, then Team and security, then Add member. Enter hello@taal.solutions and choose the Administrator role. We use it to connect your app and set up your products; we never see your members' card numbers.

  6. Add your logo and colours

    Settings, then Branding: add your logo, icon and brand colour, so receipts and payment pages look like you.

All done?Tick Stripe off under Accounts in your portal, and we'll take it from there. Stuck on a step? Reply to any of our emails or write to hello@taal.solutions.

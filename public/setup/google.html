Setup guide: Google Play

Your app on Google Play, in your name

Google charges a one-off $25 (about £20). As an organisation, you'll use the same D-U-N-S number as for Apple, so do Apple's step 2 first.

  1. Sign in with the Google account you'll keep for the business

    Go to play.google.com/console/signup. Use an account you'll keep, ideally one for the business rather than a personal Gmail.

  2. Choose an organisation account

    Choose Organisation if your studio is a limited company (you'll need your D-U-N-S number), or Yourself if you're a sole trader. Organisation accounts can publish straight away; new personal accounts have to run a two-week test with 12 people first, which we can help with.

  3. Enter your details and pay

    Fill in your organisation's details exactly as on your D-U-N-S record, a contact email and phone, and pay the $25. Google then verifies your identity and organisation, which can take a few days.

  4. Add us so we can publish your app

    In Play Console, go to Users and permissions, then Invite new users. Enter hello@taal.solutions, give Admin (all permissions) on the account, and send. You can remove us at any time.

All done?Tick Google Play off under Accounts in your portal, and we'll take it from there. Stuck on a step? Reply to any of our emails or write to hello@taal.solutions.

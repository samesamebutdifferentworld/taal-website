How much does studio booking software cost in the UK?

By Tim Allan, founder of taal, who ran a wellness studio in London for seven years. Updated 16 November 2026.

The short answer. Most independent UK studios pay between £80 and £180 a month for booking software, before card fees. Add a branded app and it's often £150 to £300 a month. Over three years that's roughly £3,000 to £11,000, and the bill keeps coming for as long as you use it.

The costs that aren't on the pricing page

  • Card fees. Every system charges for taking payments. Stripe's standard UK rate is 1.5% plus 20p a card payment; some platforms run their own payments and charge more. On £8,000 a month of memberships, half a percent is £480 a year.
  • Growing out of a tier. Prices that rise with active members mean a good year costs you more in software.
  • Add-ons. Branded apps, text messages, marketing tools and extra locations are often priced separately.
  • Contracts and price rises. Check the notice period and how much prices have risen at renewal before.
  • Switching. Moving your members out later can mean paying for a data export. See how to move from Mindbody.

Work out your real cost over three years

Take your last invoice, not the pricing page. Add the monthly fee, any app or add-on fees and VAT if it's charged on top, then multiply by 36. Add a year of card fees for each year. That number is what you're really comparing. A few examples from published prices, before card fees:

The other way to pay: once

A newer option is to pay once to have a system built for you, in your own name and accounts, then pay only running costs. With taal, a booking system is £1,900 and your own apps are £1,000 more, or spread over 12 or 24 months. Hosting and email then cost about £30 a month, paid straight to those companies, and an optional care plan costs £30 to £40 a month from year two.

To be straight about it: paying once costs more at the start, and over three years it isn't the cheapest choice if all you need is basic booking. A booking system with your own apps comes to about £5,200 over three years, including hosting, app store fees and the care plan. That's less than TeamUp or Momence with their apps and about the same as bsport, and from year four it's about £920 a year, including Apple's fee. What to ask any supplier

  • What will I pay a month at my member numbers, including VAT?
  • What do you charge to take a card payment and a Direct Debit?
  • Is the app in my name, or am I listed in yours?
  • How long is the contract, and what did prices rise by last year?
  • If I leave, how do I get my members, memberships and payment details out, and what does it cost?

How to move from Mindbody without losing members

By Tim Allan, founder of taal, who ran a wellness studio in London for seven years. Updated 16 November 2026.

The short answer. Export your clients, memberships, credits and bookings yourself from Mindbody's reports. Decide whether to move card details processor to processor or ask members to add them again. Then switch on one date, with each member's renewal date carried across, and tell members two weeks before.

1. Check your contract first

Find your agreement and note the notice period and end date. Plan to go live on the new system just before you stop paying Mindbody, so there's no month with nothing to book on, and no month paying for both.

2. Export what you can yourself

Mindbody's own reports can be exported to Excel for free. Menu names change from time to time, but these are the ones to look for under Reports:

  • Clients, then Mailing Lists: set the list to all clients and the opt-in status to all clients. This is everyone's name, email and phone.
  • Clients, then Members: who has an active membership, when they joined, what they pay and their next payment date.
  • Clients, then Visits Remaining: class packs and credits, with the option to include expired pricing options ticked.
  • Staff, then Schedule at a Glance: upcoming appointments and bookings.
  • Sales: payment history, for your records and your accountant.

If a big export times out, run it for shorter date ranges and join the files.

3. Decide about card details

Card numbers never come out in a spreadsheet. You have two choices:

  • Processor to processor. Mindbody offers a paid subscriber data export that can pass stored card details securely to your new payment processor. Other software companies report it at around $500 and five working days, varying by region. Stripe's migrations team can receive the data and attach each card to the right member.
  • Members add them again. When members first sign in to the new app, they add a card or set up a Direct Debit. For most small studios this is simpler and costs nothing, as long as you give people a clear date and a reason to do it.

4. Set up memberships to match

Recreate each membership and pack at the same price, then carry across each member's next payment date and remaining credits. Nobody should pay twice in a month or lose a class they've paid for. Check a handful of members by hand against the export.

5. Tell members early

  • Two weeks before: say what's changing and when, that their membership and credits come with them, and how to get the new app.
  • A few days before: send the link to sign in with the same email, and ask them to add a card if needed.
  • On the day: a reminder at the desk and on screen, and someone on hand to help.

6. Switch day

  • Stop Mindbody's automatic payments for the next cycle, so the new system takes the next payment.
  • Check the timetable, the first bookings and the first payments as they come in.
  • Keep your exports somewhere safe. You'll want them for your accounts and if anyone queries a payment.

If you move to taal, we do the import as part of set-up: members, memberships and credits come across, and we check them with you before you go live.

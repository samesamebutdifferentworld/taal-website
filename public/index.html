Questions

Costs, moving across, data and apps
What does the £60 a month include?

£30 a month to us for updates, fixes and email support, usually answered within a working day. It also covers keeping store apps up to date when Apple and Google require it. The other £30 or so is hosting and email, paid directly to the hosting and email providers. New features or custom work are quoted separately, so you only pay for what you ask for.

Why do I pay for hosting and email directly?

Your system runs on your own accounts, so you can see exactly what it costs and nobody adds a mark-up. Hosting stays the same even for very large studios. Email is what grows: about £30 covers up to roughly 2,000 active members. Above that it adds about £3.50 a month per extra 100 members, so about £95 a month all in at 3,000 and about £165 at 5,000. Switching reminder emails to free app notifications brings that down.

How long until we switch?

You get a working demo within a week and your full system to test within four weeks. You go live when you're happy with it and your current contract allows, usually after a notice period. Existing card and Direct Debit details can't always be transferred from your current provider. If they can't, we email your members a simple link to set up payment again and follow up with anyone who hasn't.

Can you move us from our current system?

Yes. We bring across your members, memberships and credits as part of setup, so your members carry on where they left off.

How do you look after our members' data?

Your system runs on your own hosting account, not a database shared with other studios. Health and injury notes are only visible to the staff you choose. Cards and Direct Debits are handled by Stripe, so card details never touch your system. And you can export or delete a member's data when they ask, as UK GDPR requires.

What if we stop the monthly plan?

The plan runs for at least 12 months from when you go live, then month to month with 30 days' notice. The software is licensed while the plan runs. If it stops, you get 30 days' grace, then the system becomes read-only: bookings and payments pause, but you can still sign in and export all your data.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It's yours. It lives in your own accounts and you can export it any time. If you ever leave, it leaves with you.

Who owns the apps?

You do. They're published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, in your studio's name. Apple charges about £79 a year and Google $25 once.

What about card fees?

Stripe charges its standard rates on each payment. We don't add anything on top.

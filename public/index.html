Questions

Costs, moving across, data and apps
What do we pay after setup?

Only the running costs: about £30 a month for hosting and email, paid to the providers from your own accounts. That's about £1 a day. With your own apps, Apple also charges about £79 a year and Google $25 once. There's no monthly fee to us.

How do we pay for setup?

Pay once, in three parts: a third when you sign, a third when we hand over your test version and the rest when you go live. Or spread it over 12 months: £400 to start then £150 a month for the booking system (£2,200 in total). Your own apps add £200 then £85 a month, and the website £200 then £65 a month, so all three come to £800 then £300 a month (£4,400). Our first three studios get 30% off setup, in return for a short case study.

Who sets up the accounts?

We do, in your studio's name and on your own billing: hosting, email, payments and your Apple and Google developer accounts. Apple asks businesses for a D-U-N-S number, which is free, and we'll help you get one.

Why do I pay for hosting and email directly?

Your system runs on your own accounts, so you can see exactly what it costs and nobody adds a mark-up. Hosting stays the same even for very large studios. About £30 a month covers studios with up to roughly 2,000 active members. Email is what grows beyond that, so for bigger studios we use a bulk email service and quote for your size.

How long until we switch?

You can build a working demo yourself in about a minute, and your full system is ready to test within four weeks of signing. You go live when you're happy with it and your current contract allows, usually after a notice period. Existing card and Direct Debit details can't always be transferred from your current provider. If they can't, we email your members a simple link to set up payment again and follow up with anyone who hasn't.

Can you move us from our current system?

Yes. We bring across your members, memberships and credits as part of setup, so your members carry on where they left off.

How do you look after our members' data?

Your system runs on your own hosting account, not a database shared with other studios. Health and injury notes are only visible to the staff you choose. Cards and Direct Debits are handled by Stripe, so card details never touch your system. And you can export or delete a member's data when they ask, as UK GDPR requires.

What happens after the first year?

Your licence is permanent, so the system is yours to keep using. Setup includes 12 months of updates, fixes and support. After that you can take the care plan at £600 a year, or ask us for updates when you need them. If you have your own apps we recommend it, because Apple and Google require regular updates. App updates that Apple or Google require are £150 each without the care plan.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It's yours. It lives in your own accounts and you can export it any time. If you ever leave, it leaves with you.

Who owns the apps?

You do. They're published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, in your studio's name. Apple charges about £79 a year and Google $25 once.

What about card fees?

Stripe charges its standard rates on each payment. We don't add anything on top.

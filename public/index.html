Questions

What does the £30 a month cover?

Updates, fixes and email help, usually within a working day. Studios with store apps pay £10 a month more, as Apple and Google need regular updates. New features or custom work are quoted separately, so you only pay for what you ask for.

Why do I pay Cloudflare and Resend directly?

Your system runs on your own accounts, so you can see exactly what it costs and nobody adds a mark-up. For most studios it comes to about £30 a month. Hosting stays the same even for very large studios. Email is what grows: about £30 covers up to roughly 2,000 active members, and larger studios pay a little more as they send more booking emails.

Can you move us from our current system?

Yes. We bring across your members, memberships and credits as part of setup, so your members carry on where they left off.

What happens to our data?

It is yours. It lives in your own accounts and you can export it at any time. If you ever leave, it leaves with you.

Who owns the apps?

You do. They are published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, in your studio’s name.

How long until we are live?

You see a working demo in your brand within a week and go live within four. Store apps usually follow within five to eight weeks, depending on Apple and Google approving your accounts.

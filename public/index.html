Questions

What does “about £60 a month, all in” include?

£30 a month to us for updates, fixes and email support, usually answered within a working day. It also covers keeping store apps up to date when Apple and Google require it. The other £30 or so is hosting and email, paid directly to Cloudflare and Resend. New features or custom work are quoted separately, so you only pay for what you ask for.

Why do I pay Cloudflare and Resend directly?

Your system runs on your own accounts, so you can see exactly what it costs and nobody adds a mark-up. Hosting stays the same even for very large studios. Email is what grows: about £30 covers up to roughly 2,000 active members. Above that it adds about £3.50 a month per extra 100 members, so about £95 a month all in at 3,000 and about £165 at 5,000. Switching reminder emails to free app notifications brings that down.

Can you move us from our current system?

Yes. We bring across your members, memberships and credits as part of setup, so your members carry on where they left off.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It's yours. It lives in your own accounts and you can export it any time. If you ever leave, it leaves with you.

Who owns the apps?

You do. They're published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, in your studio's name. Apple charges about £79 a year and Google $25 once.

What about card fees?

Stripe charges its standard rates on each payment. We don't add anything on top.

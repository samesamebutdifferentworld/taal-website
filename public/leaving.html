Leaving taal.

You can stop working with us whenever you like. Your system keeps running, because it was never ours. This is exactly what you keep and how to take your data with you.

What you keep

  • The systemYour licence is permanent once setup is paid. The booking system, back office and website keep working after we've gone.
  • Hosting and emailThey run on Cloudflare and Resend accounts in your studio's name, on your own billing. Nothing to move.
  • PaymentsYour Stripe account is yours. Members' cards and Direct Debits stay set up, so payments carry on.
  • Your appsThey're published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, so they stay in the stores under your name.
  • Your web addressThe domain is registered to you.
  • Your dataMembers, memberships, bookings and payments live in a database in your own account, not one shared with other studios.

How to export it

You don't need us for this. In the back office, open Import & export and download:

  • Clients, with contact details
  • Memberships
  • Bookings
  • Payments, for any dates you choose
  • A sales journal for Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, with VAT split out

They're CSV files, which open in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets and can be imported by other booking systems. If you'd like the whole database as one file, ask and we'll send it, or download it yourself from your Cloudflare account.

What we do when you go

  • Remove our access to your accounts, and confirm in writing when it's done.
  • Hand over anything we hold for you: logins we set up, your brand files and a copy of your data.
  • Delete our own copies once you've confirmed you have everything.

Stopping the care plan

The care plan runs a year at a time. Tell us 30 days before it renews and it stops; there's nothing else to cancel. Without it your system keeps running, but you won't get updates or support. If you have your own apps, Apple and Google ask for regular updates, so you'd need someone to do those.

Before you sign

We'd rather you knew this now than found out later. If anything here is unclear, ask us on the call, or read how we compare on cost.

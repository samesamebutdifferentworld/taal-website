Leaving taal.It stays yours.

Stop working with us whenever you like. Your system keeps running, because it was never ours.

What you keep

  • The systemYour licence is permanent once setup is paid. It keeps working after we've gone.
  • Hosting and emailAccounts in your studio's name, on your own billing. Nothing to move.
  • PaymentsYour Stripe account is yours. Cards and Direct Debits stay set up.
  • Your appsPublished under your own Apple and Google accounts, so they stay in the stores.
  • Your web addressRegistered to you.
  • Your dataIn a database in your own account, not one shared with other studios.
  • The codeIf we stop trading or can't support you for 90 days, you get the full source code, and any developer can look after it for you.

How to export

You don't need us for this

In the back office, open Import & export and download your clients, memberships, bookings, payments for any dates, and a sales journal for Xero, QuickBooks or Sage with VAT split out.

They're CSV files: they open in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets, and other booking systems can import them. For the whole database as one file, ask us, or download it from your Cloudflare account.

When you go

What we do
  • Remove our access to your accounts, and confirm it in writing.
  • Hand over what we hold for you: logins we set up, your brand files, a copy of your data and an up-to-date handover document, so another developer can take over in a day.
  • Delete our own copies once you have everything.

The care plan

Stopping it

It runs a year at a time. Tell us 30 days before it renews and it stops; there's nothing else to cancel. Your system keeps running without it, but without updates or support. If you have your own apps, Apple and Google ask for regular updates, so you'd need someone to do those.
